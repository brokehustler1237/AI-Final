Mini Food Delivery Assistant

A courier is taking an order from the restaurant to a customer across the city: the 6×6 neighbourhood from the project brief, or a larger 10×10 or 14×14 map with a river and several bridges. The traffic service reports on the bridge along the route, before departure or while the courier is already driving. Three algorithms decide what the courier should do, and then you see what really happened on the road.
